Sampled instruments
-------------------
All recorded instrument samples in this folder come from two public-domain libraries by Versilian Studios,
both released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication — free for any use,
including commercial use, with no attribution required). We credit them anyway, with thanks:

  VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition (VSCO 2 CE)
    Recorded by Sam Gossner & Simon Dalzell; sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote
    https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
    Used for: flute, oboe, clarinet, bass clarinet, bassoon, trumpet, trombone, French horn, tuba,
              violin, string section, pizzicato strings, cello, double bass, harp

  Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)
    https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL
    Used for: grand piano (Steinway B), harpsichord, pipe organ / reed organ, harmonica, tenor saxophone,
              xylophone, marimba, vibraphone, and the Acoustic Kit drums

For DAWG HAUS the samples were thinned to at most every 3 semitones, trimmed, normalized, and re-encoded
to 96 kbps mono MP3.

Made by DAWG HAUS
-----------------
Acoustic, nylon and electric guitar and electric bass are generated by DAWG HAUS's own plucked-string
(Karplus-Strong) model. All synthesizers, the electronic drum kits (808, 909, Trap, Boom Bap, Lo-Fi,
Electro, Studio, Latin), the metronome sounds and the dog growl are synthesized in code.
